Lexia helps creators register their work, find lawyers they can trust, and detect infringement in one place. I led UX/UI from interviews to high-fidelity screens, and our team reached the Top 20 at the Chula LegalTech Hackathon Year 7.

01 Process

Interviews with lawyers and creators came before any wireframes.
