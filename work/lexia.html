What does “proof of creation” mean?

Something that shows the work existed on a date. A layered draft, a dated sketch or an email to yourself all count.

The problem

Thai creators own copyright the moment they make something, but proving it, finding the right lawyer and spotting copies are all slow, paper-heavy and full of legal terms.

What I did

Led UX/UI for our hackathon team: interviews with lawyers and creators, the three core journeys and the pitch prototype. After the event I redesigned the product from scratch.

Result

Top 20 out of all teams at Chula LegalTech Year 6. The screens on this page are the post-hackathon redesign.

01 · Problem

Copyright is automatic. Proving it isn’t.

Under Thai law a work is protected as soon as it is made. The trouble starts when a creator needs to show it is theirs.

01Registration feels like a legal exam

Creators can record their work with the Department of Intellectual Property, but the forms use terms like “proof of creation” and “power of attorney” with no explanation.

02Lawyers are found by word of mouth

Most creators we spoke to had no way to tell which lawyers actually handle copyright, or how fast they reply.

03Copies are found by accident

Creators usually learn their work was copied when a friend sends a link. By then the evidence may be gone.

02 · Research

What lawyers and creators told us

Short interviews with IP lawyers and working creators before we drew anything.

Creators

They stall at the documents step

Registration was abandoned halfway, mostly because creators couldn’t tell what counted as proof.

Lawyers

First calls go on basic questions

Clients arrived with incomplete files. A clear checklist before the first call would save both sides time.

Both

Similar doesn’t mean stolen

A detection tool must not call everything infringement. It should help people collect evidence and ask an expert.

03 · Core journey

Register, monitor, act

Three features, one loop. Registering a work is what makes detection and legal help possible later.

01Register the work with a guided checklist
02Lexia keeps a dated record of the filing
03Detection checks the web for close copies
04A copyright lawyer reviews the evidence
04 · Redesign

Three screens, rebuilt after the hackathon

The version we pitched was rushed and crowded. Afterwards I redesigned the three core screens from our research notes, one decision at a time.

Guided registration

  • Plain words next to legal ones

  • Only the documents that apply

    The checklist changes with the type of work, and optional items say when they are needed.

  • Progress you can trust

    Four steps and a time estimate, so the form feels finishable.
