Signify

Translates sign language into text and speech in real time.

RoleUX/UI designer
TypeMobile · HCI
YearAug – Sep 2024
AwardCreative Award, ACM SIGCHI Bangkok
01 · Research

Ordering food or seeing a doctor depends on someone knowing sign language

The idea came from people I know who are hearing- and speech-impaired.

Need

Fast, accurate translation. A conversation can’t wait.

Challenge

Users differ in sign fluency and comfort with technology.

Opportunity

A module that helps non-signers learn.

02 · Design & recognition

Creative Award, Bangkok ACM SIGCHI 2024

A small design system with a warm palette, so the app feels friendly rather than clinical. A follow-up survey rated it highly on speed, clarity and communication.
